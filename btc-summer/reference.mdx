## Usage Notes

- **Token denoms** are used for IBC transfers and protocol interactions
- **Supervault addresses** are the contract addresses for liquidity provision
- **Oracle addresses** provide price feeds for the respective assets
- All addresses are on the Neutron network unless otherwise specified

<Note>
These addresses and denominations are specific to the BTC Summer campaign implementation on Neutron mainnet.
</Note>
