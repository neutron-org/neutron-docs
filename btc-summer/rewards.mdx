# Reward System Overview

The BTC Summer campaign distributes **up to $10M in NTRN rewards** to participants through a sophisticated points-based system that rewards both deposit size and ecosystem engagement.

## Base Rewards

All participants earn base rewards calculated using this formula:

$$
\text{Points} = \text{USD Value of Deposit} × \text{Days Staked} × (1 + \text{Bonus Boost})
$$

Where:
- **USD Value of Deposit** = token amount × USD price each day
- **Days Staked** = number of full days the deposit remained in the vault
- **Bonus Boost** = any additional multipliers from advanced mechanisms

Your final reward share is calculated as: `(Your Points / Total Points) × Total Rewards`

## Advanced Reward Mechanisms

Beyond base rewards, participants can significantly increase their earnings through two advanced mechanisms:

### 1. NTRN Locking Boost

Lock NTRN tokens for up to 4 years to earn substantial reward multipliers:

**How it works:**
- Choose your lock amount and duration (up to 4 years)
- Receive a transferable receipt NFT representing your locked position
- Your boost is calculated based on your locked NTRN relative to your BTC deposits

**Boost Formula:**
$$
\text{Boost} = \min(2, \frac{\text{NTRN}_{\text{Locked}}}{\text{TVL}_{\text{Deposited}}} × \frac{\text{Duration}_{\text{user}}}{\text{Duration}_{\text{max}}} × 2)
$$

**Key Benefits:**
- Up to **2x multiplier** on all rewards
- Receipt NFTs are transferable (tradeable on secondary markets)
- Locked NTRN is liquid staked with Drop for additional yield
- Long-term alignment with Neutron ecosystem

<Note>
The boost calculation ensures that larger stakes relative to deposits and longer lock periods provide maximum multipliers.
</Note>

### 2. NFT Collection Boosts

Own NFTs from Neutron-based collections listed on Superbolt to earn additional multipliers:

**Boost Tiers:**
| Threshold | Multiplier | Tier |
|-----------|------------|------|
| 1 NFT | +1% | Wooden |
| 1% of collection | +2% | Bronze |
| 5% of collection | +3% | Silver |
| 15% of collection | +4% | Gold |
| 30% of collection | +5% | Platinum |

**Collection Rules:**
- Only NFTs from collections listed on **Superbolt on Neutron** count
- Users can earn boosts from up to **5 collections**
- If eligible for more than 5 collections, the highest boosts are selected
- Rarity within collections doesn't affect boost amount

**Example:**
If you hold 600 CEWTs (>30% of collection), 50 Quarks (1% of collection), and 1 each of three other collections, you'd receive:
- +5% (CEWTs) + 2% (Quarks) + 1% + 1% + 1% = **+10% total boost**

## Wallet Linking

Since deposits can happen on Ethereum (via vaults) or Neutron (direct), but boosts only apply on Neutron, users must "link" their addresses:

**The Process:**
1. When activating NTRN or NFT boosts, specify which address holds your BTC deposits
2. This creates a "pointer" connecting your Neutron-based boosts to your deposit address
3. The system calculates your total rewards using both deposit and boost information

**Best Practices:**
- **Consistency is key**: Use the same deposit address throughout the campaign
- **Ethereum deposits**: Point all boosts to your Ethereum address
- **Neutron deposits**: Point all boosts to your Neutron address
- **Avoid splitting**: Don't deposit on both networks unless necessary

<Warning>
Splitting deposits across Ethereum and Neutron addresses can create complications in reward calculations. Choose one primary address for the best experience.
</Warning>

## Multiplier Stacking

All eligible boosts stack together for maximum rewards:

**Example Calculation:**
- Bob locks NTRN for a +100% multiplier
- Bob holds NFTs providing +4% total boost
- Bob's final multiplier: **+104%**
- This doubles his share of the total reward pool

## Maximizing Your Rewards

**For Maximum Efficiency:**
1. **Lock NTRN early** - The earlier you lock, the more days you benefit from the multiplier
2. **Acquire strategic NFTs** - Focus on collections where you can reach higher percentage thresholds
3. **Maintain consistent deposits** - Keep your BTC derivatives deposited throughout epochs
4. **Use one primary address** - Avoid complications by consolidating on either Ethereum or Neutron

**Advanced Strategy:**
Users often buy and lock NTRN specifically to dilute other participants' rewards while maximizing their own share of the pool.

The reward system integrates seamlessly with Neutron's infrastructure:
- **NTRN locking** uses liquid staking with Drop for yield optimization
- **NFT verification** connects with Superbolt marketplace data
- **Cross-chain tracking** handles deposits from both Ethereum and Neutron
- **Real-time calculations** provide live reward projections
